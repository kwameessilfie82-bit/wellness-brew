## Consent template (reviews and customer photos)

Send this on WhatsApp before you reuse a customer's photo or words anywhere outside the website:

```
Hi {name}, thank you for the lovely message! 💛
May we share your review/photo on our website and social media with your first name and town?
Reply YES to agree. You can ask us to remove it at any time.
```

Reviews submitted on the website already include this consent, through a required checkbox.
